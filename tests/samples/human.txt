Went to the market on Tuesday with my uncle. The fish seller near the bus stand had run out of sardines by nine, so we bought mackerel instead, which my aunt grumbled about all evening. Rain started around four. The roof over the back verandah still leaks in the same corner it always has, and nobody has called the carpenter even though we said we would after Onam. Tomorrow I need to renew the bike insurance before the office closes at five.
